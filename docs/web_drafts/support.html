Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why does ConsistencyGrid require Accessibility Permission?

This permission is required only if you enable our digital well-being features ("Reel Control"). It allows our app to measure vertical scroll sweeps inside Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat, or TikTok to check if you've crossed your video boundaries. We do not gather, record, or upload any personal data.

2. My wallpaper stopped updating. How do I fix it?

Aggressive battery management software on OEM devices (especially Xiaomi, OnePlus, Samsung, and Huawei) may occasionally stop background generators. To resolve this:
• Open ConsistencyGrid Settings → Look at OEM Autostart Settings and toggle it on.
• Disable battery optimization for ConsistencyGrid (set to "Unrestricted" in app info).

3. How do I delete my account?

You can easily delete your account and all associated backups:
• In-App: Go to settings, scroll down to "Account" and tap Delete Account.
• Web Portal: Submit an email request via our Account Deletion Form.
