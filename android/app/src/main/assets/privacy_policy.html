Privacy Policy for Reel Counter

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Summary: Reel Counter is built with a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, user content, messages, keystrokes, or media from your device. All tracking data stays 100% on your device.

1. Accessibility Service Usage

Reel Counter uses Android's AccessibilityService API exclusively to help users monitor and limit their short-form video consumption (digital wellbeing).

  • What We Detect: Scroll events (`TYPE_VIEW_SCROLLED`) and window state transitions (`TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED`) strictly inside specified short-form video applications (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat Spotlight, TikTok).
  • What We Do NOT Collect: We do NOT capture, read, or inspect screen content, text messages, private chats, passwords, user photos, video content, or audio.
  • Scope: Accessibility events are filtered strictly by package name (`com.instagram.android`, `com.google.android.youtube`, `com.snapchat.android`, `com.zhiliaoapp.musically`). No events from other applications are ever monitored.

2. Data Storage & Security

All counters, limits, and watch-time statistics are processed locally on your device using EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256-GCM encryption). No analytics or tracking data is uploaded to remote servers.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Accessibility Service usage, please contact us at: support@consistencygrid.com
